Running an ecommerce brand shouldn't mean rebuilding the same report every Monday

Most growing ecommerce businesses in LA and NYC are stitched together across several platforms — a storefront, one or two marketplaces, an accounting tool, maybe a fulfillment system — and none of them talk to each other. The result is almost always the same: someone on the team spends hours every week pulling exports, pasting them into Excel, and rebuilding the same pivot table so leadership can see how the business is actually doing.

Disconnected platforms

Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, and QuickBooks each tell a different piece of the story, and none of them reconcile automatically.

Manual reporting hours

Weekly or monthly reporting eats real staff time that could go toward growth instead of data entry.

Numbers that don't match

Different team members' spreadsheets drift out of sync, so no one fully trusts the "official" numbers.

Decisions made on stale data

By the time a report is finished, it's already a week old — too slow for fast-moving ecommerce decisions.

What working with a BI consultant actually changes

A business intelligence consultant's job isn't to hand you a prettier chart — it's to remove the manual work between your raw data and the decision you need to make. Quantivyx builds an automated pipeline that pulls from your existing platforms on a schedule, cleans and standardizes it the same way every time, and feeds it into a live Power BI dashboard. No one has to remember to export anything. The dashboard is simply always current.

This is the same approach used for enterprise reporting, scaled to fit a growing ecommerce operation — without the overhead of hiring a full internal data team. See how the pipeline works.
